About us

Ann’s Baking Co. was born from the passion and creativity of its founder, Anna Singh.

For Anna, baking is more than making desserts. It is a way to express creativity, bring people together and make everyday moments feel special.

We believe the sweetest memories are made around a table filled with delicious treats. Our goal is to bring that feeling to every order, whether it is a simple box of cookies or a custom cake for a special celebration.

Thank you for being part of our baking journey.

Meet the Founder

Anna Singh, Founder and Baker

Hi, I’m Anna Singh, the founder of Ann’s Baking Co.

My passion for baking comes from the joy of creating something special with my own hands. I love experimenting with flavours, exploring creative designs and turning simple ingredients into delightful treats.

For me, baking is about the happiness a cake brings to a birthday, the excitement of sharing a box of cookies, and the little moments that become beautiful memories. Ann’s Baking Co. is my way of sharing that passion with you.
